## Bacteriophage biology

- **[EnterococcusPhageEvolution](https://github.com/KWhitesonLab/EnterococcusPhageEvolution)** —
  Directed evolution of phage cocktails against susceptible and recalcitrant
  *E. faecalis*, with recombination and host-range analysis.
  Data: [PRJNA982167](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA982167)
  > Dunham SJB, Shapiro JW, Adams ED, et al. *Accelerated evolution of
  > Enterococcus bacteriophage cocktails.* Science Advances (in review).

- **[STM_phage_cocktail](https://github.com/amonsiba/STM_phage_cocktail)** —
  Genomic analysis and kinetic profiling of a three-phage cocktail against
  *Stenotrophomonas maltophilia*
  > Monsibais AN, Tea O, Ghatbale P, Dunham SJB, Zünd M, Phan J, et al.
  > Antimicrob Agents Chemother 69(3):e0116224 (2025).
  > [doi:10.1128/aac.01162-24](https://doi.org/10.1128/aac.01162-24)

- **[phage_genomics](https://github.com/eadams762/phage_genomics)** —
  Genomic characterization of phage candidates for phage therapy (Adams)

## How we share data and code

Every repository accompanying a publication should have:
a **LICENSE** (MIT or BSD-3), a **Zenodo DOI** for permanent archiving,
and **raw sequencing data in the NCBI SRA** with the BioProject accession
cited in the paper.
